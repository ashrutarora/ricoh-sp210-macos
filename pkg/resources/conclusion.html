All done

If the printer is not connected yet

Plug it in and switch it on. macOS adds it by itself, with this driver, after a few seconds.

To print

In any app choose File > Print and pick Ricoh SP 210SU as the printer.

To scan

  1. Put the page face down on the glass and close the lid.
  2. Open Image Capture (in Applications), or in Preview choose File > Import from Scanner.
  3. Pick Ricoh SP 210SU on the left and click Scan.

To print from an iPhone or iPad

Turn on System Settings > General > Sharing > Printer Sharing and tick the printer (click the ⓘ next to Printer Sharing). It then shows up in the phone’s Share > Print while this Mac is awake and on the same Wi-Fi.

If something does not work

  • macOS says “Background Items Added”: that is the scanner service. Leave it switched on in System Settings > General > Login Items & Extensions, or scanning stops.
  • You see two Ricoh printers: use the one called “Ricoh SP 210SU (macOS driver)”. You can remove the other in System Settings > Printers & Scanners.
  • Image Capture shows “Waiting for scanner” or error -9921: quit Image Capture and open it again.

To remove everything, open Applications > Utilities > Uninstall Ricoh SP 210SU.
