Ricoh SP 210SU driver for Mac

Ricoh does not make a Mac driver for the SP 210 series. This installs one so you can print from any app and scan with Image Capture or Preview.

Before you start

  1. Connect the printer to this Mac with its USB cable and switch it on. (You can also do this after installing.)
  2. Click Continue, then Install. Your Mac asks for your password: it is the one you use to log in to this Mac.

What gets installed

  • Printer driver: the printer appears as “Ricoh SP 210SU (macOS driver)”. If macOS has already added it as “RICOH SP 210SU” with a Generic PCL driver (which cannot print on this model), the installer removes that copy.
  • Scanner service: a small background program that lets Mac apps use the scanner. It only works on this Mac; nobody else on your network can see it.
  • Uninstall Ricoh SP 210SU, in Applications > Utilities, to remove it all later.

Works on Macs with Apple chips and Intel Macs, on macOS 13 Ventura or later. Tested on the SP 210SU; the SP 210, 211 and 211SU should work too. Free and open source: github.com/ashrutarora/ricoh-sp210-macos
